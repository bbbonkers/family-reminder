Family Reminder Bot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Family Reminder Bot is an SMS reminder service that sends automated session reminders, schedule updates, attendance notifications, and confirmations to users who have opted in to receive text messages.

Users receive text messages only after providing consent. Message frequency varies. Users may opt out at any time by replying STOP and may request assistance by replying HELP.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to send reminder messages, such as names, phone numbers, and session details.

How Information Is Used

Your information is used only to send session reminders and related notifications.

Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on scheduled sessions.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.

Opt Out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance.
